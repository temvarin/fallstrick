The word lists, Rektion entries and example sentences were written for this app. Levels, genders, plurals and
prepositions were checked against the sources listed in `content/sources.json` (Goethe-Institut word lists, Wiktionary,
Duden, DWDS, Grammatik aktiv, Aspekte neu); no text from these sources is reproduced.
